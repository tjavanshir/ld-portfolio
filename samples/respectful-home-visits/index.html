Respectful Home Visits

A practical Canvas course for staff who contribute to accessibility modifications in people’s homes. It develops respectful communication, consent-aware observation, safe preparation and clear handover habits.

Learning need

Help teams act respectfully and communicate clearly

Home visits involve private spaces, personal preferences and technical questions that may sit outside an individual worker’s role. Learners need practical language and repeatable steps for preparing, asking permission, documenting observations and following through.

Learning outcomes

Learners practise confirming visit details, introducing themselves and the purpose, asking before entering or recording, describing observations neutrally, and assigning follow-up to the right role.

Design approach

Short, scannable pages combine guidance with realistic decision points. Warm callouts distinguish important practice reminders, and clear section cards help learners navigate the content.

Learning journey

Four steps from preparation to handover

01 · PREPARE

Before you go

Confirm what is known, identify missing information and follow workplace safety procedures.

02 · INTRODUCE

At the door

Explain who you are and why you are there; ask how the person wants to take part.

03 · EXPLORE

Consent and boundaries

Ask before observing or photographing, respect preferences and pause when a decision exceeds your role.

04 · HAND OVER

Close the loop

Separate what was said, what was observed, what was agreed and what needs a next action.

Assessment and transfer

Practise decisions, then produce a usable handover

Scenario quiz

A Canvas quiz checks judgement across preparation, communication, consent and escalation scenarios.

Field note to handover

Learners write a concise, factual handover from a fictional visit. Canvas accepts a text response or an uploaded DOCX/PDF file.

Design principle: the practice makes role boundaries explicit. Learners record observable information and route technical decisions to the appropriate team member.
