# Selected Unit 1 design evidence

These manuscript excerpts show how **Hello, English!** moves from curriculum intent to classroom activity and evidence. Page numbers refer to the production manuscript.

## Unit plan excerpt

1. Greetings, core cast and numbers 1–3.
2. Classroom/movement language and line control.
3. Name exchange and personal name card.
4. Hello/goodbye contrast and numbers 4–5.
5. Phonics **l, t, i, s** and teacher-modelled word building with **sit**.
6. Picture story: the children help the little bird find its nest.
7. Song, badge and fine-motor consolidation.
8. Integrated review, Can Do performance and picture dictionary.

## Teacher’s Book excerpt

### Lesson 3 — What’s Your Name?

**Student Book:** 10–12  
**Activity Book:** 6–7  
**Core language:** What’s your name? I’m ___. Nice to meet you.  
**Evidence:** learner completes at least one supported name exchange.

#### 0–6 minutes — Name circle
Pass a soft object. Say I’m [name]. Each learner supplies or repeats their name.

#### 6–16 minutes — Mia and Leo dialogue (core)
Use page 10 and Track 07. Point to each speaker, repeat by role and match bubbles.

#### 16–27 minutes — Zara and Sam dialogue (core)
Use page 11 and Track 08. Sequence the mini-pictures and practise in two class groups before pairs.

#### 27–36 minutes — Partner exchange
Pairs complete the exchange. Stand to greet a new partner if routines allow, or wave to a partner across the circle.

#### 36–42 minutes — Name card
Begin page 12. Draw the face, trace/copy the name and add one simple decoration pattern.

#### 42–45 minutes — Exit exchange
Ask selected learners What’s your name? Accept I’m … or My name is …

**Optional consolidation:** finish and decorate name card; Activity Book pages 6–7.  
**Support:** learner supplies name only after teacher says I’m.  
**Extend:** learner independently asks a second partner.

## Assessments excerpt

Sample during Lesson 8 using Student Book pp. 25–26 and Activity Book p. 15. Observe pairs or small groups and make a brief individual note where needed.

1. Greet a teacher or peer.
2. Ask “What’s your name?” and answer with a name. Swap roles and allow a model if needed.
3. Follow two movement instructions.
4. Count one pictured set from 1–5.
5. Point to two named letters or anchor pictures. Record which cue was used and draw on Lesson 5 evidence for the other letters.
6. Say goodbye.

Use evidence from the whole unit. The six learner reflection stars cover greetings/farewells, saying a name, asking a name, responding to an action, counting 1–5 and finding L, T, I and S. Record dates, a short example and the level of prompting. Save a badge or name-card sample and a tracing sample where useful.
