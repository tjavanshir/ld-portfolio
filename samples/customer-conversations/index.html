The learning need

Turn a difficult conversation into a clear next action

When service has gone wrong, staff need to listen without becoming defensive, clarify what is known, explain what happens next and capture details so the next person can continue the work.

Performance challenge

In a missed-appointment scenario, the learner must respond to frustration while the replacement date is still unconfirmed. The design focuses on a calm, useful response instead of an unsupported promise.

By the end, learners can

  • Acknowledge the concern and use focused questions to clarify it.
  • Separate confirmed facts from assumptions or details still to check.
  • Agree who will act next and when the customer will receive an update.
  • Write a concise handover that another team member can use.
Learning journey

Explain, practise, check, transfer

A short sequence moves from a reusable conversation framework into decisions and job-relevant follow-through.

01 · ORIENT

Start here

Set expectations, explain the purpose and show learners how the course fits their work.

02 · LEARN

Use the CLEAR approach

Review a practical response structure with concise guidance and a desk reference.

03 · PRACTISE

Decide what to do

Work through interactive choices and a short Australian complaint-handling video with reflection.

04 · APPLY

Check and hand over

Complete the graded knowledge check, then practise recording a useful next-step handover.

The embedded course video is “Handling customer complaints: Defusing frustration”, from the NSW Department of Education’s customer-service learning resource.

Assessment & transfer

Practice connects to the work after the course

Formative decisions

Interactive practice gives learners opportunities to consider tone, clarification and realistic commitments before assessment.

Graded skills check

A 10-question Moodle quiz checks understanding of the response principles, with an 80% pass mark.

Practical handover

A short applied task asks learners to record the issue, confirmed details, next action, owner and update timing.

Job-relevant context

Examples centre on service recovery and appointment coordination, with neutral fictional workplace details.

Native LMS delivery

Built in MoodleCloud using course pages, H5P practice, a video page, a quiz and an applied activity.

Usable reference

The CLEAR guidance is paired with a concise desk reference learners can revisit during work.
