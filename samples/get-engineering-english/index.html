GET PROGRAMWORKPLACE COMMUNICATION · WRITTEN TASK

Prepare a recommendation

Choose two workplace health and safety issues. Research possible responses, then explain why the concerns matter and recommend feasible action.

“Choose two workplace health and safety issues.”
DescribeWhat is happening? Include relevant workplace details.
SupportUse research, standards or incident evidence to explain the concern.
RecommendSet out practical steps the organisation could take.
AddressWrite clearly and formally for a manager or supervisor.

Spoken task · source-based sample

Discuss the concern

A paired role-play carries the written recommendation into a workplace conversation.

Meeting brief

Engineer: explain two WHS concerns, describe their impact and propose practical solutions.

Manager: ask questions about the evidence, feasibility or next steps.

Debrief: discuss clarity, response to questions and how the proposed action could be made more useful.

Adapted from the supplied role-play instructions. The activity calls for learners to present issues and solutions, respond to questions and debrief their performance.
