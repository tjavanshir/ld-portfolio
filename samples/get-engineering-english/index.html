TASK EXCERPT · RAISE A WHS ISSUE
Choose two workplace health and safety issues. Research possible solutions, then explain the issues, their impact and your recommendations in a clear, formal message to your superior.
01 Identify issues02 Research solutions03 Support with evidence04 Write for a manager

Spoken follow-up: learners rehearse the same purpose in a simulated manager meeting, respond to questions and debrief the clarity and effectiveness of their proposals.

DESIGN IN PRACTICE

Why this sequence works

The presentation first makes consultation barriers discussable. Learners then apply that lens to a specific WHS issue in writing and in conversation. The shift from identifying a problem to justifying a response gives language practice a concrete workplace purpose.

Based on the supplied course outcomes, Week 3 WHS presentation, “Effective Communication of WHS Issues with Superiors” task and spoken role-play brief. The six-week course materials include further work in networking, conflict resolution, innovation, analytical writing, presentations and feedback.
